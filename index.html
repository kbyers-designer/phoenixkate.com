THE WORK is real.

I write about gods and wounds and the wild places humans are afraid to look. I draw on three decades of experience in design, years of hands-on wildlife rehabilitation, and a life of being the person who figured things out the hard way because no one handed her a map.

Phoenix Kate is the publishing arm of that life. You will find shadow work rooted in goddess archetypes, mythology that does not sanitize the dark, and essays that ask the questions most wellness content runs from.

The Feathered Chronicles publishes on Substack. MoonX carries the work to broader audiences with care-forward framing. The Dark Moon Collective is the membership community for readers who want to go deeper. All of it is built for people who never needed rescuing, only the permission to be exactly this.
