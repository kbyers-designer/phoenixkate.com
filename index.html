The Reluctant Kitchen Witch Spell Deck

Kitchen magic for people who hate cooking. The magic has never required a casserole.

Twelve printable spell cards that use salt, sugar, tea, and a faucet, not a recipe. Every card has a low-spoons version, because some days the spell is just standing at the sink. Print at home, stick them on the fridge, and let the kitchen do its quiet work.

Print double-sided, flip on long edge, and cut on the solid lines, or glue the fronts and backs together if you print single-sided. Each card has an intention, a gesture, and a small version that counts.

The twelve spells: Salt at the Threshold, The Tea Bag Wish, First-of-the-Month Cinnamon, Ice Cube Cool-Down, Sugar Bowl Softener, Bay Leaf in the Wallet, Lemon in a Heavy Room, Coffee Grounds Goodbye, Running Water Rinse, Honey Before Hard Words, Rosemary to Remember, and Windowsill Moon Water.
